How can we help?

Can I place an order?

Build your bag and continue to checkout. If checkout setup is still in progress, no payment will be requested. Otherwise, save your order, follow the Send Money instructions and submit your transaction ID for manual verification.

Where can I see both sides?

Each original product photograph shows the front and back together. Open a product and select its image for the full-size photograph.

Will my bag stay saved?

Your bag and favorites are stored in this browser when local storage is available. They are not shared across devices and may be removed if you clear browser data.

How do I choose a size?

Measure a tee you own using our guide. Enter your requested size at checkout; availability will be confirmed by phone before dispatch.
